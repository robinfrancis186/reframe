Reframe Picker

Saves components you pick on your own tabs to the Reframe library on this computer. The quickest way to connect: open Reframe in this browser, go to Library → Saved components → Pick from your own browser and choose Connect extension. To connect by hand instead, choose Show a token there and paste it below.

To pick: click the Reframe Picker toolbar button (or press Alt+Shift+R) on any page, hover, click the element, then press Enter. Arrow keys move to the parent or child element; Esc cancels.
