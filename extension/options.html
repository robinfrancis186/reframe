Reframe Picker

Saves components you pick on your own tabs to the Reframe library on this computer. In Reframe, open Library → Browser extension, choose Pair extension, and paste the token here.

To pick: click the Reframe Picker toolbar button (or press Alt+Shift+R) on any page, hover, click the element, then press Enter. Arrow keys move to the parent or child element; Esc cancels.
